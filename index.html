💡 Quick Tips to Improve Your WiFi
1

Restart your router regularly

Unplug your router for 30 seconds and plug it back in. This clears memory and often fixes slow speeds and drops.

2

Position your router correctly

Place your router in a central open location. Avoid thick walls between you and the router.

3

Use the right frequency band

5GHz gives faster speeds but shorter range. 2.4GHz is slower but reaches further.

4

Disconnect unused devices

Every connected device uses bandwidth. Disconnect devices you are not using to free up speed.

5

Keep your devices updated

Outdated software and drivers can cause WiFi problems. Keep everything up to date.

6

Avoid interference

Microwaves and cordless phones can interfere with WiFi. Keep your router away from these.

7

Use a WiFi extender for large spaces

A WiFi extender or mesh network can eliminate dead zones and improve coverage throughout.
